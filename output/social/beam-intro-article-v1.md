Title: What is Beam?

Post caption:
Meet Beam. Send crypto and stock tokens with a link.

Here’s what it does and how it works.

Article:

Beam lets you send crypto and stock tokens to anyone through a link. Instead of asking for a wallet address, you create a Beam and share it in a message. The person receiving it doesn’t need to have a wallet set up beforehand.

To send one, connect your wallet, choose a supported token and enter a dollar amount. Confirm the transaction, and Beam creates your link. If a swap is needed, it’s handled as part of the sending process. Beam runs on Robinhood Chain and supports ETH, stock tokens and other supported tokens on the network.

The recipient opens the link and signs in with Apple or Google to claim. A wallet is created for them if they need one, and Beam handles the claim’s network fee. They don’t need to buy crypto for gas before receiving what you sent.

Your Beams keeps track of what you’ve sent and whether it’s been claimed. If a Beam hasn’t been claimed yet, you can cancel it and recover the tokens. Anyone with the full link can claim, so share it privately with the intended recipient.
