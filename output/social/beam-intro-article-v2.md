Title: What is Beam?

Post caption:
Meet Beam. Send stocks and crypto with a link — one asset or a Spectrum bundle.

Here’s what it does and how it works.

Article:

Beam lets you send stocks and crypto through a link. Instead of asking for a wallet address, you create a Beam and share it in a message. The recipient opens it, signs in, and claims. They don’t need an existing wallet — one is created for them when needed, and Beam handles the claim’s network fee.

To send one, connect your wallet, choose a supported asset, and enter a dollar amount. Confirm the transaction and share your link. Beam runs on Robinhood Chain and supports ETH, stock tokens, and other supported tokens on the network.

You can also send several assets together with Spectrum. Pick a preset bundle, choose an amount, and pay in ETH. Beam splits the budget by the preset’s weights, swaps it into the selected assets, and puts them behind one link. The recipient claims the whole bundle together. These are fixed bundles of tokens, without automatic rebalancing.

Your Beams tracks what you’ve sent and whether it’s been claimed. You can cancel an unclaimed Beam to recover its tokens. For Spectrum, that returns the assets in the bundle rather than converting them back to ETH. Anyone with the complete link can claim, so share it privately with the intended recipient.
