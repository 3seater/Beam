# Stock selection artwork assets

Original company logos from the site's `stockLogoUrl` provider:
`https://assets.parqet.com/logos/symbol/{TICKER}?format=png`.

Saved for the stock-selection artwork on September 13, 2026. Tesla is copied
from the existing `public/social/tokens/tsla.png`, from the same provider.
The selected tickers come from the user's stock-picker screenshot and the
site's canonical starter assets in `lib/robinhood-tokens.ts`.

The logos are raster originals; typography, glass tiles, curves, Beam mark,
and identity badges remain editable HTML/CSS/SVG. This graphic is a selection,
not an exhaustive catalog or a live availability guarantee.
